# Memorandum

**To** Board of Directors
**From** Group Chief Financial Officer
**Date** 21 June 2024
**Subject** Cost programme and reporting changes for the second half

## 1. Cost reduction target

The Board approved a target to reduce group operating expenses by **4 per cent**
against the 2024 plan in the second half of the year, with the reduction weighted
towards discretionary categories: travel, marketing and third-party consulting.
Personnel and facilities costs are out of scope for the current programme.

## 2. Americas reorganisation

Following the reorganisation effective **01 July 2024**,
the cost centre previously reported as **OPS-NA** is reported as
**OPS-AMER**. The 2024 plan has been restated onto the new code for the full
year. Prior-year comparatives have **not** been restated and remain on the former
code. Analysts should map the two codes when presenting any comparison that spans
the effective date.

## 3. Chart of accounts

Client entertainment previously recorded under Travel & Entertainment has been
reclassified with effect from the same date, to align external reporting with the
way the commercial team manages the spend. The chart of accounts carries both the
former and the current parent with validity dates; the underlying account code is
unchanged.

## 4. Freight

Expedited freight has run materially ahead of plan in the Americas following the
tooling failure on line 3 in July. Operations expect the position to normalise in
the fourth quarter. The Board asked that the full-year effect be quantified at
the next meeting.

## 5. Headcount

Headcount reporting is produced by the People team in the HR system and is not
maintained in the finance ledger. The Board's request for a cost-per-head series
will be met from the HR system separately.
