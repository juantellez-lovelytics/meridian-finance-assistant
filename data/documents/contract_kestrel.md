# Master Services Agreement — Kestrel Freight Systems

Between Meridian Instruments Inc. ("Customer") and Kestrel Freight Systems ("Carrier").
Commencement 1 January 2023. Initial term 36 months.

## 3. Charges

3.1 Carrier shall invoice Customer monthly for services performed, at the rates
set out in Schedule B.

3.2 Charges are stated exclusive of fuel surcharge, which is applied at the
index published on the first business day of each month.

## 4. Volume rebate

4.1 Where Customer's aggregate charges under this Agreement in any calendar year
exceed **USD 500,000**, Carrier shall credit Customer
**4 per cent** of the amount by which the aggregate exceeds
that threshold.

4.2 The rebate is calculated on charges net of credit memos and is settled by
credit note within 90 days of the calendar year end. Rebates are **not** applied
against invoices during the year to which they relate.

## 5. Payment terms

Net 45 days from invoice date. Customer may withhold disputed amounts provided
the dispute is raised within 20 days of invoice.

## 9. Liability

Carrier's aggregate liability is limited to the charges paid in the twelve months
preceding the claim.
