# Statement of Work — Northgate Advisory

Client: Meridian Instruments Inc. · SOW-2024-03 · effective 1 February 2024

## Services

Northgate will provide advisory support to the Operations function covering
process assessment, supplier rationalisation and reporting design.

## Fees

Time and materials at a blended rate of USD 2,150 per consultant-day. Fees are
invoiced monthly in arrears against a signed timesheet.

A not-to-exceed ceiling of **USD 480,000** applies to this SOW. Northgate will
notify the Client when cumulative fees reach 80 per cent of the ceiling.

## Expenses

Travel and subsistence are recharged at cost, subject to the Client's own Travel
& Entertainment policy. Expenses are invoiced on the same schedule as fees and
are counted against the ceiling.

## Payment terms

Net 30 days from invoice date.
