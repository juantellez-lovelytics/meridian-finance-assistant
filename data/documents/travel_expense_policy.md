# Meridian Instruments — Travel & Entertainment Policy

Version 4.2 · effective 1 January 2023 · owner: Group Controller

## Scope

Applies to all employees of Meridian Instruments Inc., Meridian Instruments
Canada Ltd. and Meridian Instruments B.V. Amounts are stated in US dollars;
where an expense is incurred in another currency, the local equivalent at the
month-end rate applies.

## Air travel

Economy class is required for all scheduled flights with a total duration under
six hours. Business class may be booked for flights over six hours with the
written approval of a Vice President.

## Lodging

Nightly room rates must not exceed **USD 275** in tier-one cities and
**USD 190** elsewhere. Tier-one cities are: New York, San Francisco, Chicago, Boston, London, Amsterdam, Toronto, Zurich.
Rates are exclusive of local tax. Where a stay exceeds the applicable cap,
the difference is not reimbursable and must be recharged to the employee.

## Meals

A per-diem of **USD 85 per day** applies to employee meals while travelling.
Receipts are not required within the per-diem.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment over **USD 500** per event requires the sign-off of a Vice
President in advance of the event. The names and employers of all attendees must
be recorded.

## Pre-approval

Any single expense of **USD 1,000 or more** requires documented pre-approval
before the commitment is made. The approval reference must be recorded against
the transaction at the time of posting. Expenses of USD 1,000 or more without an
approval reference are reported to the Audit Committee quarterly.

## Non-reimbursable

Personal entertainment, airline lounge memberships, spouse or partner travel,
traffic fines, and in-room minibar charges.
